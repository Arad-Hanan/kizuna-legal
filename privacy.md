# Kizuna Privacy Policy
*Last updated Oct 27, 2026 · Managed by @Arad*

This policy explains what personal data Kizuna (an anime card-collecting Discord game bot) collects, why it is needed, how it is stored, and your rights regarding your information. Kizuna is operated by an independent developer ("we", "us"). We only store data strictly necessary to provide and maintain the game.

---

### 1. Data We Collect
To operate the game, Kizuna stores the following limited information:

* **Discord Identifiers:** Your unique Discord User ID and Server IDs (Guild IDs) where you play, ensuring your game progress is correctly mapped to your account.
* **Game Progress & Inventory:** Your cards, card copies, levels, Ascension, Bond, currencies (coins, shards, Foxfire), active teams, quests, achievements, titles, dungeon/arena stats, wishlists, market listings, auction bids, and notification preferences.
* **Activity & Transaction History:** Logs of card pulls, trades (sender, recipient, items swapped), and currency changes. This data is used solely to resolve bugs, restore lost items, prevent cheating/alt-farming, and process support requests.
* **Vote Data:** When you vote for Kizuna on top.gg, top.gg sends us your Discord User ID and timestamp so we can deliver your in-game vote reward.
* **Server Moderation Logs:** If server staff use Kizuna's moderation suite, warning notes, reasons, moderator User IDs, and timestamps are stored for that specific server.
* **Server Settings:** Channel IDs, role IDs configured for bot commands, and message IDs posted by the bot for auto-updating menus.

*Kizuna does NOT offer real-money purchases and does NOT collect or store financial details, payment cards, real names, IP addresses, or passwords.*

---

### 2. Message Content & Discretion
Kizuna reads message content only in channels where it has permission, for three specific functions:
1. Recognizing bot command prefixes (e.g., `$`).
2. Detecting channel activity to trigger random card spawn encounters.
3. Operating an automated word filter exclusively within the official Kizuna Support Server.

**Kizuna never saves, logs, stores, or transmits the text of your personal messages.**

---

### 3. How Your Data Is Used
We use collected data strictly to:
* Save your game progress, inventory, and achievements across play sessions.
* Facilitate trades, auctions, leaderboards, and community events.
* Enforce fair play, investigate exploits, and prevent automated botting/farming.

We **do not** sell your data to third parties, use it for advertising, or track your activity outside of Discord.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services
Your data is shared only with services necessary to operate the bot:
* **Discord:** Processes all bot interactions under [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
* **top.gg:** Handles bot voting verification under [top.gg's privacy policy](https://top.gg/privacy).
* **Hosting Provider:** Database servers are hosted securely via **netcup GmbH** (located in Germany, EU), adhering to strict EU data privacy standards (GDPR).

---

### 5. Data Retention & Backups
* **Active Progress:** Your game data is retained as long as Kizuna is operational, allowing you to resume playing anytime.
* **Database Backups:** Automated database backups are created regularly for disaster recovery, kept for up to 14 days, and then deleted automatically.
* **Error Logs:** System error reports containing basic diagnostic info (User ID, Server ID, and command triggered) are posted to a private, staff-only log channel to debug technical issues.

---

### 6. Your Rights & Data Deletion
Depending on your region (including EU/UK GDPR and California CCPA rights), you have full control over your data:

* **Data Access:** You can request a copy of the personal data Kizuna holds about you at any time.
* **Data Deletion:** You can delete your data in a server by using the `$deletedata` (or `/deletedata`) command in that server. It permanently removes your profile progress, inventory, currencies, and activity history in that server; your data in other servers is not affected. To delete your data in every server at once, contact us in the support server or via email.
* **Server Data Removal:** Server owners may request the complete removal of all bot data associated with their Server ID by contacting support.
* **Notifications:** You can disable DMs and pings at any time using in-game settings (`$notifications`) or by adjusting your Discord privacy settings.

---

### 7. Children's Privacy
Kizuna complies with Discord's Terms of Service. Users under the minimum age required to use Discord in their country (13+ in most regions) are not permitted to use Kizuna. If we become aware that data belonging to a minor under the eligible age has been collected, it will be promptly deleted.

---
