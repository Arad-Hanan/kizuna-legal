* **What is sold:** The Foxfire Shop offers Foxfire packs, bundles, a cosmetic Supporter Pack and the Fox Pass monthly membership. Prices, contents and limits are shown in the Foxfire Shop (in the Kizuna Market) and on our Ko-fi page before you buy.
* **Who you pay:** Purchases are made on Ko-fi and paid through PayPal or another method Ko-fi offers. Ko-fi and the payment provider process your payment under their own terms; we never see your card or bank details.
* **Delivery:** Paste your shop code from the Foxfire Shop at Ko-fi checkout and your items are added to your profile in the server where the code was made, usually within a few minutes, with a receipt by DM. If you forget the code, use **Claim a purchase** with the transaction ID from Ko-fi's receipt email, or contact us.
* **Fox Pass:** The Fox Pass renews every month until you cancel it on Ko-fi. Each paid month gives its Foxfire at once and 30 days of extra Foxfire on daily claims. Cancelling stops future payments; the days already paid for still run.
* **Virtual items only:** Foxfire, bundles and the Supporter Pack are virtual items under Section 2: they have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money, stay in the server they were delivered to, and cannot be transferred, except as the game itself allows.
* **Random rewards:** Foxfire can be spent on limited banners, which give random cards. The odds and pity of every banner are shown in `/banners` under **Rates and pity**.
* **Refunds:** Because items are delivered right away, purchases are final once delivered, and by buying you ask for immediate delivery and accept that you lose any right to withdraw from the purchase once it is delivered, where the law allows this. A purchase that was not delivered, was delivered twice by mistake, or could not be delivered (for example a once-per-player item bought twice, or a bundle bought after its sale ended) is refunded. Email us within 14 days with your Ko-fi transaction ID. This does not limit any refund rights you have under the law of your country.
* **Chargebacks:** If a payment is reversed or charged back, we remove the items it delivered.
* **Belgium:** Foxfire packs, bundles and the Fox Pass are not offered to players in Belgium. Players there can buy only the Supporter Pack.

### 5. Adding Kizuna to Your Server
* **Who can add it:** Anyone with the Manage Server permission can add Kizuna to a Discord server and set it up with `/kizuna setup`. Server managers choose which channels Kizuna uses, whether it posts news from Kizuna (and who that news pings), and which extras are on, such as the welcome line and Kizuna's moderation tools. They can change these at any time with `/kizuna settings`.
* **Servers run their server, we run the game:** Server staff cannot give out currency or cards, or change drop rates, prices, events or rewards. Only Kizuna's developer changes the game.
* **One schedule for everyone:** Arena seasons and limited banners run on one schedule shared by every server.
* **Progress stays in its server:** Your cards, currencies and other progress in one server do not carry over to another server.
* **Moderation tools:** Kizuna's moderation tools only act when a server's staff use them, and that server's staff are responsible for how they use them.
* **Removing Kizuna:** If Kizuna is removed from a server, that server's data is kept for 30 days in case it is added back, then deleted, as described in the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
